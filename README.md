## Biography

In 2025 I graduated with a Bachelor's degree in Physics from the Universidad Complutense de Madrid (UCM). Throughout my undergraduate journey, I developed a strong foundation in quantum mechanics and statistical physics, which sparked my fascination with collective phenomena, non-linear dynamics, and complex systems. 

Currently, I am pursuing my Master's degree at UIB, where I am tailoring my research toward advanced data science, network theory, and deep learning architectures (including RNNs, LSTMs, and GRUs). My Master's Thesis (TFM) explores predictive modeling in mobility networks, blending physical network principles with state-of-the-art machine learning frameworks.

---

## My research

My research focuses on the intersection of Network Science, Deep Learning, and Complex Systems, particularly within the domain of predictable transport dynamics and air traffic management. I am currently dedicated to developing an AI-driven framework for real-time flight delay propagation across airport networks. By leveraging Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), my work aims to capture the intricate topological and temporal dependencies of global aviation infrastructure.
